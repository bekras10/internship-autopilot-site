Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Internship Autopilot ("the app") is a personal, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Google API scopes and why they are used

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly: read-only access to the owner's Gmail, used to identify recruiting-related messages (for example application confirmations, assessment invitations, interview requests, rejections, and verification codes for the owner's own job applications). The app cannot send, delete, or change email.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets: used to read and update the owner's own internship application-tracker spreadsheet.

Information stored

  • Public internship postings (company, title, location, link, dates).
  • Records of the owner's own applications (position, date, status).
  • Limited metadata from relevant recruiting emails (such as sender, subject, date, and a classification like "interview" or "rejection"). Full email contents are not retained beyond what is needed for that classification.
  • OAuth tokens that let the app access the owner's account.

All data is stored on the owner's private workstation, in files whose access permissions are restricted to the owner. The app has no public server, website backend, or user database.

How information is used

Data is used only to provide the app's features to its owner: finding internships, avoiding duplicate applications, tracking application status, and alerting the owner to recruiting messages that need attention.

Sharing

  • Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, and it is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • The app communicates with Google APIs to provide the features above. When the owner applies to a job, the employer's application website receives only the application information the owner chooses to submit. Gmail data is not sent to employers or other services.

Limited Use

Internship Autopilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner uses the app for an internship search. The owner can delete the local database and stored tokens at any time, and Google data stored by the app is deleted when the app is retired.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the app can no longer reach Gmail or Google Sheets.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be raised through the issues page of this site's GitHub repository.
